# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ruwaifatahir/ai-launchpad/security/advisories/new).

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact. Expect a reply within a few days.

## Contracts

The contracts are the official Pons V2 contracts, copied unchanged from the verified source on Robinhood Chain mainnet, where they run live. They have not been independently audited. Deploying them is your decision and your risk.

Report bugs in the contracts themselves to [Pons](https://ponsfamily.com) as well.
